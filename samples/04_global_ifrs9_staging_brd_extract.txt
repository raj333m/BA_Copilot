Project: IFRS 9 Staging Data Feed Enhancement
Client: Group Finance - Credit Risk Reporting
Context: IFRS 9 impairment / ECL - credit risk data
Systems: Source: loan origination system, card platform, collections mart. Target: ECL engine input feed (monthly, CSV via SFTP). DW: Teradata risk mart. Current SICR logic held in an Excel macro owned by Credit Risk.

BUSINESS REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (EXTRACT) - DRAFT v0.3

1. Background
The monthly ECL engine input is prepared partly by hand. Stage allocation (Stage 1 / 2 / 3) is computed in an Excel macro using days past due and a lifetime PD comparison supplied by the models team. Last two quarter-ends the feed was delivered late (WD+6 against a WD+3 target) and external audit raised a finding on lack of controls and lineage.

2. Objectives
- Move stage allocation logic into the risk mart so it is automated, repeatable and auditable.
- Deliver the ECL input feed by WD+3.
- Full lineage from source to feed for each account.

3. Requirements (as provided by Credit Risk)
3.1 Stage 3 if 90+ DPD or credit-impaired flag (default, forbearance with financial difficulty, insolvency).
3.2 Stage 2 if significant increase in credit risk: 30+ DPD backstop, OR lifetime PD at reporting date > 2.5x origination lifetime PD (threshold to be confirmed by Model Risk), OR on watchlist.
3.3 Otherwise Stage 1.
3.4 Cure / probation: account to remain in Stage 2 for 3 consecutive months after criteria no longer met before moving to Stage 1 (TBC).
3.5 Feed fields: account id, product, segment, balance, EAD, stage, stage reason code, origination PD, current PD, DPD, forbearance flag.
3.6 Reconciliation of feed balances to General Ledger by product within tolerance (tolerance TBC by Finance).
3.7 Stage movement report month on month (stage 1->2, 2->3, cures) for the Impairment Committee.

4. Open points
- Origination PD missing for ~3% of legacy card accounts - fallback approach not agreed.
- Who signs off the feed each month - Finance or Credit Risk?
